## 🎯 **New AI Features**

### 1. **AI Financial Coach Chat** 💬

A floating AI coach button appears on your dashboard!

**How to Use:**

- Click the "🤖 AI Coach" button in the bottom-right corner
- Chat opens with a friendly AI financial expert
- Ask ANY question about finance:
  - "What is compound interest?"
  - "How do I start investing?"
  - "Explain budgeting in simple terms"
  - "What's the difference between stocks and bonds?"

**Features:**

- ✅ Real-time responses
- ✅ Personalized to your progress
- ✅ Simple, easy-to-understand explanations
- ✅ Real-world examples
- ✅ Available 24/7

---

### 2. **Smart Quiz Hints** 💡

Get AI help during quizzes WITHOUT giving away the answer!

**How to Use:**

- While taking a quiz, click "💡 Ask AI for Help"
- AI analyzes the question and gives you a helpful hint
- Hint guides you toward the right answer
- Learn the concept, don't just memorize

**Example:**

- Question: "What is inflation?"
- Hint: "Think about how prices change over time and what happens to your purchasing power..."

---

### 3. **AI-Enhanced Explanations** 📚

Wrong answers become learning opportunities!

**How it Works:**

- Get a question wrong? No problem!
- AI rewrites the explanation in a personalized, encouraging way
- Adds helpful tips and real-world examples
- Makes learning from mistakes easier

---

### 4. **Progress Analysis** 📊

AI analyzes your learning journey!

**How to Use:**

- Click "📊 Analyze My Progress" in the AI chat
- AI reviews your:
  - Points and level
  - Completed levels
  - Current streak
  - Virtual balance
- Get personalized recommendations on:
  - What to study next
  - Where to improve
  - Study strategies

### 5. **Quick Actions** ⚡

Pre-built helpful prompts!

**Available Quick Actions:**

- 📊 **Analyze My Progress** - Get detailed progress analysis
- 💡 **Study Tips** - Get personalized study recommendations
